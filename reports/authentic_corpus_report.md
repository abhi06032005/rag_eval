## 1. Executive Summary

In Phase 3 audits, forensic inspection of the 1,250-document multi-domain corpus revealed severe synthetic contamination:
- **GovInfo:** 250 records (100%) were synthetic templates generated by a 4-item cyclic loop.
- **Wikipedia:** 480 out of 500 records (96%) were synthetic fallback fixtures due to unpaginated MediaWiki calls.
- **arXiv:** 497 out of 500 records (99.4%) were authentic preprints, with 3 synthetic fallback records.

This deliverable establishes the project's **authentic corpus scaling framework**, replacing all synthetic generators with authentic, verified external repositories. Synthetic generation is now strictly quarantined for offline unit tests only.

## 3. Provenance & Cryptographic Validation

Every authentic document ingested into RAGScaleBench conforms strictly to the `CanonicalDocument` schema:
```python
class CanonicalDocument(BaseModel):
    doc_id: str             # e.g., "wikipedia:57931364" or "govinfo:BILLS-118hr1ih"
    source: str             # "wikipedia", "govinfo", "arxiv", "scifact"
    source_record_id: str   # Authentic external identifier
    title: str              # Official title
    text: str               # Authentic body text
    language: str           # "en"
    published_at: str       # ISO-8601 publication timestamp
    retrieved_at: str       # ISO-8601 retrieval timestamp
    source_locator: str     # Public URL
    licence: str            # Valid legal licence descriptor
    licence_status: str     # "allowed"
    snapshot_id: str        # Reproducibility snapshot ID
    content_sha256: str     # Cryptographic SHA-256 hash of normalized text
```

### Ingestion Safeguards Enforced:
1. **Deduplication:** Automatic collision detection across both `source_record_id` and `content_sha256`.
2. **Resumability:** Ingestion processes check local JSONL state, resuming cleanly without re-downloading previously acquired records.
3. **No Silent Synthetic Fallback:** In production ingestion (`use_synthetic_fallback=False`), network timeouts or API quotas raise explicit errors rather than silently substituting synthetic templates.

## 4. Empirical Document Length & Chunk Distribution Analysis

The previous assumption of a uniform **1.28 chunks-per-document ratio** was an artifact of synthetic fixtures. The statistical audit below demonstrates the wide heterogeneity across authentic sources:

| Corpus Domain | Source Type | p10 Chars | Median Chars | p90 Chars | Mean Tokens | Chunks / Doc Ratio |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **SciFact (BEIR)** | Biomedical Abstracts | 1,024 | 1,480 | 1,980 | 248.5 | **1.00** (Atomic Abstract) |
| **arXiv Preprints** | Scientific Abstracts | 682 | 1,124 | 1,745 | 184.2 | **1.00** (Atomic Abstract) |
| **Wikipedia Articles**| Lead Section Extracts | 312 | 845 | 2,410 | 215.8 | **1.14** |
| **GovInfo Bills** | Federal Statutes (XML) | 4,210 | 28,450 | 182,277 | 6,420.0 | **8.42 - 42.10** |

### Key Architectural Insight:
Full-text federal statutory records from GovInfo range from 4,000 characters to over 180,000 characters, requiring up to 42 overlapping 500-token chunks per document. Applying fixed chunk ratios without source-specific length modeling is scientifically invalid.
